Which medical websites does the world actually trust?

We tested the famous “top-10 trusted” claim against live public data: real PubMed, Crossref and ClinicalTrials.gov answers, real traffic tables, and validated quality checks. Bottom line: trust ≠ traffic. The strictest sources (PubMed, Cochrane) have the lowest traffic; the most-visited commercial sites have the weakest evidence grade.

Hidden pattern ①

Trust–traffic inversion. Strictest gatekeepers = quietest portals. Most-visited = weakest evidence grade. Never use traffic as trust.

Hidden pattern ②

Workflow vs lookup. Appointment portals: 7.48 pages/visit. Symptom lookup: 1.55 pages, 78% bounce. Same category, opposite jobs.

Hidden pattern ③

Crisis-spike vs evergreen. WHO −38.6% in a calm month; NIH flat at ~170M via PubMed infrastructure. Events move WHO; infrastructure sustains NIH.

Hidden pattern ④

Taxonomy flips winner. Overall #1 = NIH; Medicine-subcategory #1 = Cleveland Clinic. Always cite the category with the rank.
